# SMAA 1x lookup tables

`area_tex.rg8` and `search_tex.r8` are the 160x560 RG8 and 64x16 R8 tables from
the official [iryoku/smaa](https://github.com/iryoku/smaa) implementation. The byte
payloads were extracted from its `Textures/AreaTex.h` and `Textures/SearchTex.h`.
They are distributed under the accompanying MIT `LICENSE.txt`.
